How Butterfly Works

3 simple steps to stream your mobile apps directly to Discord.

STEP 1
Instant Pairing
Open the Butterfly desktop companion, scan the QR code with your phone, and pair securely in seconds without any login.
STEP 2
Set Privacy Rules
Choose which apps to share on Discord. Sensitive apps like WhatsApp, Banking, and Messages are blacklisted by default.
STEP 3
Live Discord Status
Whenever you open YouTube, Spotify, or Netflix on your phone, your Discord status updates instantly in real time!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Butterfly.

Is Butterfly safe and privacy-friendly?
Yes! Butterfly never reads your chat messages, photos, passwords, or location. Sensitive applications like WhatsApp, Banking, and Photos are blacklisted by default and never transmitted.
Does this violate Discord Terms of Service?
No. Butterfly uses Discord's official local IPC Rich Presence client API (the exact same protocol used by games like League of Legends or Spotify on PC).
Do I need to sign in with my Discord account?
No! You never enter your Discord login or password. Butterfly pairs anonymously using a 6-digit QR code token.
